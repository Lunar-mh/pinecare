# User Agreement

## Version
- Version: 2026.03-live-defense
- Effective date: 2026-03-12

## Service description
This backend powers a smart elderly-care platform with health archive, medication, emergency response, family collaboration, voice interaction, AI assistance, and medical resource integration.

## User responsibilities
- Provide truthful information for care and emergency workflows
- Use AI consultation and companion features only as supportive tools
- Do not misuse replay, volunteer, or admin capabilities outside authorized scenarios

## Platform responsibilities
- Keep APIs available, auditable, and compatible with contest defense workflows
- Clearly distinguish live integrations from stub or fallback behavior
- Protect sensitive information and only expose desensitized evidence

## Emergency and medical clause
- SOS, fall detection, and medical integration support faster coordination but do not replace medical institutions, emergency services, or clinical judgment
- When the backend reports stub or fallback mode, that state must be treated as non-live support capability
