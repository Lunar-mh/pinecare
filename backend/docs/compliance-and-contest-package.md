# Compliance And Contest Package

## Contest fit
- Recommended category: software application and development.
- AI is presented as an enabling capability, not the sole judging axis.

## Submission checks
- Verify the work is original and all third-party assets are authorized.
- Confirm there are 2-5 authors from the same school.
- Confirm at least 2 authors can attend the onsite national defense.
- Prepare a stable web entry, demo account, or replay evidence package.

## Sensitive-data controls
- Phone numbers, ID cards, and access tokens must stay masked in logs and evidence.
- Medical reports, location traces, and emergency contacts must be presented in desensitized form.
- Seed data must be labeled as competition baseline data, not live production evidence.

## Required texts
- Privacy policy
- User agreement
- Medical disclaimer
- AI usage notice
- Third-party services and authorization list
- Data desensitization note

## External services to document
- DashScope
- WeChat login and subscribe message APIs
- Aliyun SMS
- OSS upload path
- Medical upstream provider
- Map/location provider
