# Medical Disclaimer

## Version
- Version: 2026.03-live-defense
- Effective date: 2026-03-12

## Core statement
The medical directory, appointment, consultation, and report capabilities are assistance tools for scheduling and information access. They are not a diagnosis engine and do not replace licensed medical professionals.

## Live and fallback distinction
- `effectiveMode=live` means the backend is currently using a configured upstream path
- `effectiveMode=stub` or `fallbackUsed=true` means the response is replay, cache, or competition data support only
- Contest defense materials must explicitly preserve this distinction

## Safe-use rules
- High-risk symptoms must be escalated to emergency or offline care channels
- AI outputs must be presented with medical caution and human follow-up recommendations
- Report samples and replay assets must not be mislabeled as live clinical evidence
